# Storylines

Picked 2026-11-14: **A. The empty door.** The other two stay here so a later turn can switch.

## A. The empty door (picked)
- Device and form: a moment in someone's day, on a locked camera with graphics on the real ground.
- One angel stands alone at the Slush entrance, days before the event. The 2,989 attendees
  appear as name tags on the empty forecourt; Grabbit leaves only the shortlist.
- First two seconds: a wide, still shot of the entrance, one person, back to camera.
- Beats: alone at the door → the crowd of names → tabs over the doors → the laptop opens →
  into the screen: the real app → away, only the shortlist tags stay → the alert → through
  the door → end card.
- Look: real footage in cold daylight; names and tabs in the app's white panels and violet.
- Sound: wind and near silence, music from the moment the laptop opens, ElevenLabs voice.

## B. The team at the door
- Device and form: the four team members speak to camera, short documentary with cutaways.
- "We came to Slush four days early." The team speaks at the entrance, shows the app on a
  laptop, and the alert interrupts them.
- First two seconds: one team member, close, mid-sentence, the entrance behind.
- Beats: claim to camera → the number over the building → app capture with reactions →
  the alert interrupts → last line to camera → end card.
- Look: handheld, natural, clean titles beside the speaker.
- Sound: the team's real voices, light music.

## C. The rabbit run
- Device and form: the camera is the rabbit; one fast, low run from the street to the doors.
- Each stop on the run is one step of the product; the logo's speed lines trail the camera.
- First two seconds: the ground rushes past, the entrance far ahead.
- Beats: the run starts → thesis → question → shortlist → alert → the doors fill the frame →
  end card.
- Look: wide lens, motion blur, app captures cut in hard at each stop.
- Sound: fast music, cuts on the beat.
